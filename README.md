## 🎮 About WARVEX

**WARVEX** is an upcoming Third-Person Shooter (TPS) game developed by **AZAF Studio**, using the Unity 6.6 game engine.

Built around third-person combat, tactical encounters, enemy AI, weapon mechanics, and immersive gameplay systems, WARVEX aims to deliver an engaging shooting experience.

This repository is the official development space for WARVEX, where its gameplay systems, source code, assets, and future updates will be maintained.

> ⚠️ WARVEX is currently under development. Features listed below represent planned development goals and are subject to change.

---

## ⚔️ Planned Features

### 🏃 Player Mechanics
- Third-person character controller.
- Walking, running, and sprinting.
- Jumping and movement mechanics.
- Smooth camera follow system.
- Aiming and character positioning.

### 🔫 Combat & Weapon Systems
- Third-person shooting mechanics.
- Weapon aiming and firing.
- Reloading and ammunition management.
- Weapon recoil and shooting effects.
- Multiple weapon types and switching.

### 🤖 Enemy AI
- Intelligent enemy behavior.
- Enemy detection and awareness.
- Patrol and attack mechanics.
- Combat interactions.
- Health and damage systems.

### 🎯 Gameplay Systems
- Mission-based gameplay.
- Objectives and progression.
- Player health and damage.
- Inventory and ammunition management.
- Mission completion and failure systems.

### 🎨 Visuals & Audio
- 3D environments.
- Character and weapon animations.
- Combat visual effects.
- Sound effects and background audio.
- Gameplay HUD and user interface.
